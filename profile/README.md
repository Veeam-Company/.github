## What is Veeam Backup?

**Veeam Backup** is the world's leading platform for **modern data protection**, providing backup, recovery and replication solutions for cloud, virtual, and physical environments. Developed by the trusted **Veeam company**, it delivers unparalleled reliability, speed, and security to ensure business continuity and resilience against data loss, ransomware, and downtime.

With Veeam Backup, organizations can achieve:
- **Instant Recovery** of files, applications, and entire systems.
- **Hybrid Cloud Flexibility** with seamless protection across on-premises, Azure, AWS, and more.
- **Ransomware Protection** with immutable backups and secure storage.
- **Compliance and Governance** for regulated industries.

The platform is trusted by over 450,000 customers worldwide, from SMBs to large enterprises, making **Veeam** synonymous with dependable backup. Whether you're protecting critical VMs, NAS filers, or cloud workloads, Veeam Backup offers a unified, scalable solution that simplifies complex IT environments.
